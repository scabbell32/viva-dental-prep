### **Q1. Small, white to yellow raised sebaceous glands appear as spots in hairless areas of the body. What is the name for these spots?**
* a. Fordyce granules
* b. Lichen planus
* c. Morsicatio buccarum (Cheek biting)
* d. Leukoedema

* **Correct Answer:** **A. Fordyce granules**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  Fordyce granules have a raised, yellow to white hue and are ectopic sebaceous glands. Lichen planus exhibits a white lacy appearance, morsicatio buccarum exhibits an irritated/macerated appearance, and leukoedema has a blue opalescent appearance that disappears when stretched.

---

### **Q2. Lichen planus that exhibits a white lacy appearance is termed:**
* a. Atrophic lichen planus
* b. Reticular lichen planus
* c. Erosive lichen planus
* d. Plaque-like lichen planus

* **Correct Answer:** **B. Reticular lichen planus**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  Reticular lichen planus is the most common form and characteristically exhibits Wickham striae (a white lacy appearance). Atrophic and erosive forms present with red, irritated, or ulcerative surfaces.

---

### **Q3. The varicella-zoster virus causes which of the following?**
* a. Herpes Labialis
* b. Shingles
* c. Primary Herpetic Gingivostomatitis
* d. Herpangina

* **Correct Answer:** **B. Shingles**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  Shingles (herpes zoster) is caused by reactivation of the latent varicella-zoster virus. Herpes labialis and primary herpetic gingivostomatitis are caused by HSV-1; herpangina is caused by coxsackievirus.

### **Q4. One characteristic associated with a differentiation of Fordyce granules and cheek bites is which of the following:**
* a. Both are found in all age groups
* b. When the tissue is stretched, opalescence disappears
* c. It is found in the buccal mucosa
* d. Both may have a white/yellow hue in appearance

* **Correct Answer:** **B. When the tissue is stretched, opalescence disappears**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  Intracellular edema is responsible for leukoedema and will disappear when the tissue is stretched. Morsicatio buccarum (cheek biting) exhibits macerated tissue that will not disappear upon stretching.

### **Q5. Fordyce granules are ectopic sebaceous granules and affect most adults but are not found in those who have not reached puberty. This is due to:**
* a. Chronic trauma
* b. Androgenic hormones
* c. Dental product sensitivity
* d. Due to hyperkeratosis

* **Correct Answer:** **B. Androgenic hormones**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  Fordyce granules respond to androgenic hormones during puberty, which is why both adolescent males and females begin developing them at puberty, but they are absent in young children.

### **Q6. The irritation fibroma is most often found in certain oral areas. Which choice best describes the location?**
* a. The tongue
* b. The vestibular areas
* c. Areas of high trauma such as the lips, tongue, and gingiva
* d. The soft palate

* **Correct Answer:** **C. Areas of high trauma such as the lips, tongue, and gingiva**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  Areas of high trauma such as the labial mucosa, tongue, buccal mucosa along the occlusal line, and gingiva experience chronic bite trauma leading to irritation fibromas.

### **Q7. The most common age group for an irritation fibroma is:**
* a. Adolescent ages in both males and females
* b. A 45-year-old female
* c. A 65-year-old male patient
* d. A 70-year-old geriatric female patient

* **Correct Answer:** **B. A 45-year-old female**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  Irritation fibromas are most commonly diagnosed in adult females in their 40s to 60s (such as a 45-year-old female) along the bite line over time.

### **Q8. A mucocele is not a true cyst because of which of the following?**
* a. The mucin is surrounded with granulation tissue.
* b. The mucin is surrounded with connective tissue.
* c. The mucin is not surrounded with epithelium.
* d. The salivary duct is dilated due to epithelial thickening.

* **Correct Answer:** **C. The mucin is not surrounded with epithelium.**
* **Clinical Rationale:**
  A true cyst must be lined by epithelium. Mucoceles are pseudocysts formed by spilled mucin into connective tissue lined only by granulation tissue.

* c. Ranula
* d. Sialometaplasia

* **Correct Answer:** **C. Ranula**

### **Q12. Which of the following would not be the etiology in the development of the torus palatinus?**
* a. Bacterial or viral components
* b. Genetic propensity
* c. Masticatory factors
* d. Trauma to the bone

* **Correct Answer:** **A. Bacterial or viral components**
* **Clinical Rationale:**

### **Q15. All of the following are a contributing factor in Lingua villosa (Hairy Tongue) EXCEPT one. Which one is the EXCEPTION?**
* a. Radiation to the head and neck
* b. The use of proton-pump inhibitors
* c. Excessive alcohol use
* d. Consumption of excessive xylitol products such as gum and mints

* **Correct Answer:** **D. Consumption of excessive xylitol products such as gum and mints**
